# Personal role notes

These notes were written after the project.

- I set the overall design direction and defined the key lifecycle states.
- Two product designers designed most detailed screens and prototypes.
- I led design reviews and approved the final design-QA pass before release.
- I joined engineering knowledge-transfer sessions when the state logic or edge cases needed clarification.
- Product made scope decisions. Engineering owned implementation. QA owned functional testing.
- I sometimes say "I designed ClearCase," but the work was shared and I do not want to erase the other designers' contribution.
