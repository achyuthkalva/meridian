# PermitPath intake notes

Date: 14 February 2023
Author: Service design workshop note-taker
Status: Contemporaneous notes; not approved strategy

## People in the session

- Two county permit clerks
- One review supervisor
- One resident who had submitted a building permit in the previous year
- Product manager
- Designer

## What people described

- Residents download a PDF, complete it, and email it with attachments.
- A clerk copies the details into the county case system.
- If an attachment is missing, the clerk emails the resident and moves the case to a personal follow-up list.
- Residents cannot see whether the county received their material or who needs to act next.
- Clerks said duplicate submissions are common when residents receive no reply.
- Review supervisors use a spreadsheet to track cases close to statutory deadlines.
- A resident said the hardest part was not knowing whether silence meant waiting, rejection, or a lost email.

## Known constraints

- The county case system cannot be replaced in the current budget year.
- The first release must meet the existing records-retention rule.
- Email must remain available as a fallback during the rollout.

## Open questions

- Which permit types create the highest avoidable follow-up load?
- Can the existing case system expose reliable status events?
- What evidence would show that residents understand the next step?
