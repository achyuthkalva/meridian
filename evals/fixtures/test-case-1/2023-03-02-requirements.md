# PermitPath first-release requirements

Date: 2 March 2023
Owner: Product manager
Status: Approved first-release scope

## In scope

1. A resident can start, save, and submit one residential permit type.
2. The service checks required fields and attachments before submission.
3. A clerk can see new submissions in a shared queue and assign an owner.
4. A clerk can request missing information without opening a separate email thread.
5. A resident can see received, information needed, under review, approved, and declined states.
6. Every state change records its time and staff actor.
7. The service sends an email when a resident needs to act.

## Out of scope

- Replacing the county case system
- Mobile inspection scheduling
- Payments
- Supervisor analytics beyond an export

## Delivery note

The resident service will pass approved submission data into the existing case system. Staff remain responsible for the legal decision.
